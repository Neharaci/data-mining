Q2(b) MINHASH SPACE/ACCURACY EVIDENCE
=====================================

CORPUS
------
Total corpus notices: 12000
Trusted labelled pairs: 900
Unique notices used in experiment: 1644

The labelled pairs were used as the trusted evaluation set.

MINHASH RESULTS
---------------

Signature size: 32
Storage per notice: 32 hash values
Mean absolute Jaccard error: 0.065754
Maximum absolute error: 0.297897
False merges at 0.65: 53
Missed same pairs at 0.65: 63

Signature size: 64
Storage per notice: 64 hash values
Mean absolute Jaccard error: 0.039703
Maximum absolute error: 0.167870
False merges at 0.65: 7
Missed same pairs at 0.65: 88

Signature size: 128
Storage per notice: 128 hash values
Mean absolute Jaccard error: 0.031417
Maximum absolute error: 0.123461
False merges at 0.65: 4
Missed same pairs at 0.65: 80

Signature size: 256
Storage per notice: 256 hash values
Mean absolute Jaccard error: 0.022464
Maximum absolute error: 0.112526
False merges at 0.65: 4
Missed same pairs at 0.65: 85

ADOPTED SIZE
------------
Chosen MinHash signature size: 128 hash values per notice.

REASON
------
32 hashes produced 53 false merges and was therefore too inaccurate.

64 hashes reduced false merges to 7.

128 hashes reduced false merges further to 4 and had mean absolute
Jaccard error of 0.031417.

256 hashes reduced mean error further to 0.022464, but did not reduce
the observed false merges below 4. It also doubles the signature
storage compared with 128 hashes.

Therefore 128 hashes was selected as the fixed space/accuracy
trade-off for the retrieval system.

IMPORTANT
---------
The MinHash approximation introduces measurable error compared with
exact Jaccard. At the 0.65 decision threshold, the 128-hash
approximation produced 4 false merges on the 900 trusted labelled
pairs.

The final system will use this fixed 128-hash representation unless
later retrieval-quality measurements demonstrate that the operating
point needs adjustment.
