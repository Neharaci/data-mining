ADOPTED OPERATING POINT
-----------------------
LSH retrieval threshold: 0.55
Final exact similarity threshold: 0.65

RATIONALE
---------
LSH is used only for candidate generation. Its threshold is deliberately
lower than the final similarity threshold so that likely duplicate
pairs are not discarded before exact comparison.

At LSH threshold 0.55, the candidate set is reduced from 71,994,000
possible pairs to 10,441,502 pairs, an 85.497% reduction.

The trusted labelled pairs show 79.21% survival of adjudicated same
pairs at this operating point.

A higher LSH threshold of 0.65 reduces the candidate set further but
only retains 57.35% of labelled same pairs, which represents too much
retrieval loss.

Final merging remains conservative: candidate pairs must pass the
exact normalized-word Jaccard threshold of 0.65.

ASYMMETRIC RISK
---------------
The labelled data contains 621 different pairs and 279 same pairs.
False merges are more costly than missed merges according to the
problem statement.

Therefore LSH is treated as a recall-oriented candidate generator,
while the final exact similarity threshold provides the conservative
merge decision.
